LE SSERAFIM

LE SSERAFIM is a high-energy, five-member South Korean girl group formed by Source Music and HYBE. The group consists of members Miyawaki Sakura, Kim Chaewon, Huh Yunjin, Kazuha, and Hong Eunchae. Their name is an anagram of the phrase "I'm Fearless," which reflects their core identity: an unshakable self-confidence and a determination to move forward without being swayed by the expectations or judgments of others. Since their debut in May 2022, LE SSERAFIM has built a reputation for sleek, hard-hitting choreography, bold fashion concepts, and an eclectic music style spanning pop, hip-hop, reggaeton, and rock. Rather than following traditional K-pop tropes, their songs and concepts frequently address their personal journeys, vulnerabilities, and real-life ambitions, creating a direct and authentic connection with their global fanbase, known as FEARNOT.
